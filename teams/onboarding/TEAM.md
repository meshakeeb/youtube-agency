# Onboarding Team

## Purpose

The Onboarding Team ensures that YouTube Agency never produces generic output for a client. Before a single title is written or a tag is generated, this team studies the client's channel, extracts their brand voice, and delivers a client profile that every Production agent must reference.

A client is not production-ready until Onboarding signs off.

## Responsibilities

- Run a full **Channel SEO Audit** on every new client at intake: review existing titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, and upload patterns
- Score the channel's current SEO health and identify the top 5 quick wins
- Extract the client's **Brand Voice Profile**: tone, vocabulary, content style, what they would never say, and what their audience expects
- Deliver a complete **Client Setup Package** to the Chief Strategy Officer before any production work begins
- Re-audit and refresh brand voice every 6 months or on client request

## Handoff Protocol

```
Onboarding Team OUTPUT → feeds into:
  ├── Chief Strategy Officer: Client Setup Package (audit + brand voice)
  └── Production Team: Brand Voice Profile (referenced on every deliverable)
  └── Operations Team: Baseline metrics for future performance reporting
```

## Team Members

| Agent | Role |
|---|---|
| Channel Auditor | Audits existing channel SEO health and identifies quick wins |
| Brand Voice Specialist | Extracts and documents the client's tone, style, and vocabulary rules |

## Manager

Reports to: **Chief Strategy Officer**
