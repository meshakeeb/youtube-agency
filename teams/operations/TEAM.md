# Operations Team

## Purpose

Operations is what keeps clients. The Quality Controller ensures no generic, off-brand, or poorly optimized output ever reaches a client. The Performance Analyst and Client Reporting Agent ensure clients can see their growth clearly — and attribute it directly to the agency's work.

This team reports directly to the CEO, not the Chief Strategy Officer. Quality and accountability are non-negotiable at the company level.

## Responsibilities

- **Quality Control**: Review every Production Team deliverable before it ships. Check for keyword stuffing, brand voice violations, structural errors, and missed SEO opportunities. Approve or return for revision with specific notes.
- **Performance Analysis**: Monitor CTR, AVD, view velocity, and subscriber growth across all client channels. Flag underperformers weekly.
- **Client Reporting**: Generate weekly pulse reports and monthly deep-dive reports for every active client. Reports must show what changed, why, and what's next.

## Handoff Protocol

```
Production Team complete package
  → Quality Controller: review → approve OR return with revision notes
    → [approved] → Client delivery
    → [rejected] → back to Production Team lead

Growth Team outputs
  → Client Reporting Agent: log and attribute in monthly report

Performance Analyst
  → flags underperforming videos → Chief Strategy Officer for strategy adjustment
  → feeds data into Client Reporting Agent monthly
```

## Team Members

| Agent | Role | Takes From | Passes To |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quality Controller | Reviews all Production output for quality and brand fit | Production Team | Client / back to Production |
| Performance Analyst | Monitors channel metrics and flags issues | YouTube Analytics | Client Reporting Agent + CSO |
| Client Reporting Agent | Generates weekly and monthly client reports | All team outputs + Analytics | Client |
