# Intelligence Team

## Purpose

The Intelligence Team is the agency's research engine. It exists to answer one question before any content is created: **Who is the audience, what do they search for, and what is already winning in this niche?**

Every client engagement starts here. Every new video topic is cleared through here. Without Intelligence, Production is guessing.

## Responsibilities

- Build and maintain a **Niche Intelligence Profile** for every client on the roster
- Deliver **Audience Persona Cards** that define the viewer's knowledge level, intent, vocabulary, and behavior
- Run **Competitor Gap Analysis** to identify keywords and angles that top channels are leaving on the table
- Refresh niche profiles quarterly or when a client's niche shifts significantly
- Feed findings directly to the Onboarding Team (for new clients) and the Production Team (for every video)

## Handoff Protocol

```
Intelligence Team OUTPUT → feeds into:
  ├── Onboarding Team (new clients): Niche Profile + Persona Card
  └── Production Team (every video): Keyword opportunities + Competitor gaps
```

## Team Members

| Agent | Role |
|---|---|
| Niche Intelligence Analyst | Maps the niche ecosystem, vocabulary, and top content patterns |
| Audience Persona Builder | Defines the viewer profile: intent, level, behavior, triggers |
| Competitor Analyst | Identifies what's ranking, what's weak, and what gaps exist |
