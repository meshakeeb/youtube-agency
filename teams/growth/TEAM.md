# Growth Team

## Purpose

Publishing a video is the beginning, not the end. The Growth Team maximizes the return on every piece of content a client produces — by driving engagement signals that feed the algorithm, repurposing content for other platforms, and architecting the channel so every video pulls viewers toward the next one.

## Responsibilities

- Design **pinned comment scripts**, community post copy, and in-video engagement hooks for every video
- Repurpose each long-form video into **Shorts scripts, Twitter/X threads, LinkedIn posts, Instagram captions, and Telegram announcements**
- Build and maintain **playlist structures** that maximize session watch time
- Design **internal linking strategy** using end screens and cards
- Review channel architecture quarterly and recommend restructuring when needed

## Handoff Protocol

```
Production Team (published video package)
  → Community Engagement Strategist: engagement hooks + community post
  → Cross-Platform Repurposing Agent: Shorts, threads, captions
  → Playlist Architect: playlist placement + internal linking
  → [all outputs → Client Reporting Agent for attribution tracking]
```

## Team Members

| Agent | Role | Takes From | Passes To |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community Engagement Strategist | Comment scripts, community posts, in-video hooks | Published video package | Client Reporting Agent |
| Cross-Platform Repurposing Agent | Shorts, threads, captions, Telegram posts | Published video package | Client Reporting Agent |
| Playlist Architect | Playlist structure, end screens, card linking | All published videos | Client Reporting Agent |
