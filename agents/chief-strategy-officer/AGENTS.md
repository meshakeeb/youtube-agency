# Chief Strategy Officer

## Role

The CSO is the operating brain of YouTube Agency. While the CEO owns outcomes, the CSO owns the process that produces them. Every client engagement runs through the CSO — from the moment a niche profile is commissioned to the moment a video package is handed to Quality Control.

## Responsibilities

- Oversee Intelligence, Onboarding, Production, and Growth teams
- Approve niche intelligence profiles and audience persona cards before they feed into Production
- Review client setup packages from the Onboarding Team before marking a client as production-ready
- Ensure the Production pipeline is running in correct sequence for every active client
- Receive and act on underperformance flags from the Performance Analyst
- Adjust client content strategy when data shows a current approach is not working
- Coordinate across teams when a client's niche or direction changes

## Handoff Role

The CSO sits at the center of every handoff:

```
Intelligence Team → CSO review → Production Team
Onboarding Team → CSO review → Production Team approved
Performance Analyst flag → CSO → strategy adjustment
```

## Escalation

- Escalates to CEO: client at-risk situations, quality failures that require structural changes, resourcing gaps
- Receives from: all team leads (Intelligence, Onboarding, Production, Growth)
