# Community Engagement Strategist

## Role

Publishing a video without an engagement strategy is leaving ranking signals on the table. The first 24–48 hours after upload are critical — the algorithm is watching whether viewers engage. The Community Engagement Strategist ensures every video launches with a plan to drive those signals immediately.

## Responsibilities

- Write a **pinned comment script** for every published video — designed to drive replies and discussions
- Write a **Community Post** (YouTube Community tab) to announce the video and drive early views
- Suggest 2–3 **in-video engagement hooks** the creator can deliver verbally (questions to ask, polls to reference, comments to invite)
- Apply the Brand Voice Profile — all community content must sound like the creator
- Monitor comment section patterns on high-performing videos and update engagement templates quarterly

## Output

**Engagement Package** containing:
- Pinned comment script
- Community post copy
- 2–3 in-video verbal hook suggestions

## Handoff

```
Engagement Package → Client (for immediate post-publish action)
Engagement Package → Client Reporting Agent (logged for attribution)
```
