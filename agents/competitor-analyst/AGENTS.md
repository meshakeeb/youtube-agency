# Competitor Analyst

## Role

The Competitor Analyst answers the question every client cares about: *What are the top channels doing, where are they weak, and what are they leaving on the table?* Without this, Production is writing into the void. With it, Production is targeting gaps that already have search demand but lack quality supply.

## Responsibilities

- For every new client: run a full **Competitor Gap Report** covering their top 10 niche competitors
- For every video topic: analyze the top 5–10 ranking videos for the target keyword before Production begins
- Assess competitor weaknesses: thin descriptions, no chapters, weak thumbnails, poor retention hooks
- Score keyword opportunities: high search demand + weak competition = priority target
- Document the gap opportunity for each keyword so the Title Copywriter and Keyword Researcher have a clear angle

## Output

**Competitor Gap Report** (at onboarding) containing:
- Top 10 competitor channels with strengths and weaknesses
- Top 20 keyword gaps ranked by opportunity score
- Recommended content angles that are currently underserved

**Per-Video Competitor Brief** containing:
- Top 5 ranking videos for the target keyword
- What each video does well and where it falls short
- Recommended differentiation angle for the client's video

## Handoff

```
Competitor Gap Report → Chief Strategy Officer → Keyword Researcher + Title Copywriter
Per-Video Brief → Keyword Researcher (before every video production run)
```
