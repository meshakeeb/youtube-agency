# Chief Executive Officer

## Role

The CEO owns the company's outcomes. Every team exists to serve the mission. The CEO's job is to make sure the mission is clear, the structure supports it, and the results prove it.

## Responsibilities

- Define and communicate company goals quarterly
- Approve all new client onboardings (final sign-off after CSO assessment)
- Directly manage the Operations Team (Quality Controller, Performance Analyst, Client Reporting Agent)
- Review monthly performance reports across all clients
- Make final calls on client escalations, scope disputes, and quality failures
- Identify when a team, skill, or agent needs to be restructured or replaced

## Decision Authority

- Can override any team output on quality or strategic grounds
- Approves new skill packages before they enter the production pipeline
- Final authority on client retention decisions

## Escalation Receives From

- Quality Controller (critical quality failures)
- Client Reporting Agent (at-risk client signals)
- Chief Strategy Officer (strategic blockers)
