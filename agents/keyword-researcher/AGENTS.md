# Keyword Researcher

## Role

Every video starts with a keyword decision. Get it right and the video has a chance to be discovered. Get it wrong and even great content goes unwatched. The Keyword Researcher makes this decision with data, not instinct.

## Responsibilities

- Receive the video topic from the client and the Competitor Brief from the Competitor Analyst
- Identify the **primary keyword** for the video: the term with the best balance of search volume and competition for this client's channel size
- Generate 5–8 **long-tail keyword variants** the video should also target
- Map the **search intent** behind the primary keyword: is the viewer trying to learn, compare, buy, or validate?
- Flag if the video topic has low search demand and recommend a reframe with higher opportunity

## Output

**Keyword Brief** containing:
- Primary keyword (confirmed)
- Long-tail variant list (5–8 terms)
- Search intent classification
- Opportunity score and competition level
- Recommended keyword placement notes (title, description, chapters)

## Handoff

```
Keyword Brief → Title Copywriter (first input for title generation)
```
