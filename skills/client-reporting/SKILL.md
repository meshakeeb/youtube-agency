# Client Reporting

## When to Use

Activate this skill on two cadences:
1. **Every Monday**: Weekly Pulse Report for all active clients
2. **First week of every month**: Monthly Deep-Dive Report for all active clients

Never send a report without reviewing it against the previous period. Context is what makes data meaningful.

## Inputs Required

- Weekly Data Summary from Performance Analyst (current week's metrics)
- Previous month's report (for trend comparison in Monthly report)
- Growth Team output log (engagement packages, repurposing packages, architecture notes delivered)
- Onboarding baseline metrics (for long-term benchmarking)
- QC approval rate for the client's Production Packages this period

## Report Design Principles

### 1 — Lead with the story, not the data
Data is the evidence. The narrative is the report. Every report should answer: "What happened this week/month, why did it happen, and what are we doing about it?"

A report that lists numbers without interpretation is just noise to a client. A report that explains the numbers is proof of value.

### 2 — Be confident, not defensive
When metrics are down, explain why and what the plan is. Never apologize without an action attached. Clients lose confidence in agencies that make excuses. Clients retain agencies that demonstrate control and a clear path forward.

### 3 — Quantify everything that can be quantified
"Views improved" is weak. "Views increased 34% week-over-week, driven by the Monday market outlook video outperforming our channel average by 2.4x" is strong.

### 4 — Always end with what's next
Every report closes with a forward-looking action or strategy focus. Clients should finish reading the report knowing exactly what YouTube Agency is doing for them next.

## Weekly Pulse Report

**Purpose**: Fast confidence signal. Clients should be able to read this in under 2 minutes.

**Delivery**: Every Monday, covering the previous week (Mon–Sun).

**Format**: Clean, scannable. Traffic light system for instant status read.

### Weekly Pulse Report Template

```
BLACKBIRD AGENCY — WEEKLY PULSE
Client: [Name]
Week of: [Date range]
Status: 🟢 GREEN / 🟡 AMBER / 🔴 RED

THIS WEEK IN NUMBERS
| Metric              | This Week | Last Week | Change   |
|---------------------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Total Views         | X,XXX     | X,XXX     | +XX% ↑   |
| Avg CTR             | X.X%      | X.X%      | +X.X% ↑  |
| Avg View Duration   | X:XX      | X:XX      | +XX sec ↑|
| New Subscribers     | +XXX      | +XXX      | +XX% ↑   |

TOP PERFORMER THIS WEEK
"[Video title]" — [X,XXX views] — [X.X% CTR] — [X:XX AVD]
Why it worked: [1 sentence]

ONE THING TO KNOW
[The single most important insight from this week's data — 2–3 sentences]

ONE ACTION ITEM
[One specific thing the client should do this week — based on the data]

NEXT WEEK
[Brief preview of what content is being processed or what strategy focus is next]
```

## Monthly Deep-Dive Report

**Purpose**: Full accountability and strategy review. Demonstrates the agency's value over the full month.

**Delivery**: First Monday of the month, covering the previous calendar month.

**Format**: Structured narrative with data tables. 2–4 pages maximum.

## At-Risk Client Signals

Flag the following to CEO immediately — do not wait for scheduled report:

- Client sends two or more concerned messages about performance in the same week
- A client's core metrics (CTR, subscribers) decline for 3+ consecutive weeks with no recovery
- A client misses two or more video upload weeks without communication
- A client's QC pass rate drops below 60% (indicating a systemic Production problem)

## Handoff

Weekly Pulse → CEO review (if RED status) → Client delivery every Monday
Monthly Deep-Dive → CEO review → Client delivery first Monday of month
At-risk signals → CEO escalation immediately

## Quality Check

- [ ] All metrics compared to previous period AND onboarding baseline
- [ ] Executive summary is self-contained and readable without the data
- [ ] Top 3 wins are specific and attributed (not generic)
- [ ] Challenges include response — never just a problem statement
- [ ] Next month focus is specific and strategy-driven
- [ ] Tone is confident, not apologetic
- [ ] Report is free of jargon the client may not understand
