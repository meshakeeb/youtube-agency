# YouTube Title Writing

## When to Use

Activate this skill after the Keyword Brief has been approved. Takes its place as the second step in the Production pipeline. Do not begin title writing without:
- Confirmed primary keyword (from Keyword Brief)
- Approved Brand Voice Profile
- Approved Audience Persona Card

## Inputs Required

- Keyword Brief (primary keyword, intent type, long-tail variants)
- Brand Voice Profile (tone, vocabulary rules, ALWAYS/NEVER rules)
- Audience Persona Card (click triggers, language rules, knowledge level)
- Per-Video Competitor Brief (what angles top results already use — to differentiate)

## Title Formula

Every title follows this structure:

```
[Primary Keyword] + [Modifier] + [Benefit / Hook]
```

**Primary Keyword**: the confirmed keyword from the Keyword Brief — must appear in the first 50 characters
**Modifier**: adds specificity, recency, or context — examples: "in 2025", "for beginners", "step-by-step", "honest", "complete guide"
**Benefit or Hook**: the reason the viewer should click — answers "what's in it for me?" — examples: "and actually profit", "without losing money", "that top traders use", "the truth they hide"

## CTR Principles

**Click triggers to use** (match to Audience Persona Card):
- Curiosity gap: imply you know something they don't ("What no one tells you about...")
- Specificity: specific numbers outperform vague claims ("7 setups" beats "multiple setups")
- Recency: year or "right now" signals current relevance
- Social proof: "what professionals actually do", "used by top traders"
- Fear of loss: "mistakes that cost you money", "what to avoid"
- Aspiration: "how I turned $1K into $10K", "from beginner to profitable"

**What to avoid**:
- Vague hooks with no specificity ("Amazing Trading Tips!!")
- Misleading promises the video cannot deliver
- Keyword stuffing (multiple keywords crammed unnaturally)
- All-caps aggression (unless this is explicitly in the brand voice)
- Excessive punctuation (!!!, ???)

## Length Rules

- **Target**: 50–70 characters
- **Minimum**: 40 characters (too short = missed keyword opportunity)
- **Maximum**: 100 characters (YouTube hard limit; only use >70 chars for complex topics)
- **Primary keyword**: must be within the first 50 characters
- **Case**: match the client's established style — Title Case or Sentence case (never mixed)

## Search Intent Alignment

Match title framing to the keyword's intent type (from Keyword Brief):

| Intent Type | Title Framing |
|---|---|
| Informational | "How to [do X]", "[X] Explained", "Complete Guide to [X]" |
| Comparative | "[X] vs [Y]", "Best [X] in 2025", "[X] Review: Honest Opinion" |
| Validation | "Is [X] Worth It?", "The Truth About [X]", "Why [X] Actually Works" |
| Navigational | Lead with channel/creator name, then topic |

## Writing Process

### Step 1 — Draft 6–8 raw title ideas
Generate freely without judging. Include:
- At least 2 curiosity gap angles
- At least 2 direct benefit angles
- At least 1 fear-of-loss angle
- At least 1 social proof angle

### Step 2 — Filter against rules
Remove any title that:
- Violates a NEVER rule from the Brand Voice Profile
- Places the primary keyword after position 50
- Is misleading or the video cannot deliver on it
- Sounds identical to a top competitor's title

### Step 3 — Select the best 3
Choose:
- **Primary**: highest CTR potential with strong keyword placement
- **Alternative A**: different emotional angle, same keyword
- **Alternative B**: different hook style, same keyword

### Step 4 — Character count check
Count characters for each title. Flag any over 100.

## Output Format

```
TITLE SET
Client: [Name]
Video Topic: [topic]
Primary Keyword: [keyword]
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

PRIMARY TITLE (recommended):
"[Title]"
Character count: XX
Keyword position: characters 1–XX
Hook type: [Curiosity / Benefit / Fear / Social proof]
Why: [1 sentence rationale]

ALTERNATIVE A:
"[Title]"
Character count: XX
Angle: [how this differs from Primary]

ALTERNATIVE B:
"[Title]"
Character count: XX
Angle: [how this differs from Primary and Alternative A]

BRAND VOICE CHECK:
[ ] No NEVER rules violated
[ ] Vocabulary matches Brand Voice Profile
[ ] Sounds like this creator, not a generic SEO title
```

## Handoff

Title Set → Description Writer (Primary Title confirmed as working title)

## Quality Check

- [ ] Primary keyword in first 50 characters of all 3 titles
- [ ] All titles between 50–70 characters (or justified if longer)
- [ ] Brand Voice Profile NEVER rules verified
- [ ] No title duplicates a top competitor's framing
- [ ] Intent alignment confirmed
- [ ] All 3 titles use different angles
