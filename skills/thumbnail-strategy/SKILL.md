# Thumbnail Strategy

## When to Use

Activate this skill as the final step in the Production pipeline, after the Chapter Set has been delivered. All prior outputs (title, description, tags, chapters) should be available as context — the thumbnail must visually reinforce what the title promises.

## Inputs Required

- Primary Title (confirmed from Title Set)
- Audience Persona Card (click triggers, knowledge level, visual preferences)
- Niche Intelligence Profile (what thumbnail styles perform in this niche)
- Video topic and key angle (from client or chapter structure)

## The Thumbnail's Job

A thumbnail has one job: earn the click before the viewer reads the title. On mobile — where over 70% of YouTube viewing happens — a thumbnail is displayed at approximately 168 × 94 pixels. If it doesn't communicate clearly at that size, it has failed.

The thumbnail and title work as a unit. The thumbnail stops the scroll. The title closes the click.

## Niche Visual Conventions

Different niches have established visual languages. The Thumbnail Strategist must know the niche's conventions and either match them (to signal belonging) or deliberately break them (to stand out). Always document which strategy is being applied.

**Trading / Finance**: dark backgrounds, bold text overlays, charts or currency symbols, serious expression
**Fitness**: bright backgrounds, before/after implication, high-energy expression, body-visible frame
**Tech review**: product-forward layout, clean white or dark backgrounds, comparison side-by-side
**Education**: face-forward with expression, text overlay with key number or concept, warm tones
**Vlog / Lifestyle**: candid or high-emotion face, location visible, minimal text

## Brief Components

### 1 — Layout Direction
Specify the spatial arrangement of the three core elements: subject (face or object), text overlay, and background.

Common layouts:
- **Face left, text right**: classic; face draws the eye, text delivers the hook
- **Face right, text left**: reversal; works when the text needs to be seen first
- **Full-face close-up, text bottom**: high emotional impact, expression-forward
- **No face, object-forward**: works for product reviews, chart analysis
- **Split screen**: best for comparison or "before/after" videos

### 2 — Text Overlay
YouTube best practice: maximum 5 words on a thumbnail. More text = smaller font = unreadable on mobile.

Text overlay rules:
- Maximum 5 words (hard limit — if the brief calls for more, revisit)
- Must be readable at 168 × 94px (phone thumbnail size)
- Bold, high-contrast font against the background
- Never use thin or script fonts for primary text
- Text must complement the title, not repeat it exactly

### 3 — Color Palette
Specify 2–3 colors: dominant, accent, and text color.

Color psychology by niche:
- **Trading**: deep navy/black + gold or green (wealth, seriousness)
- **Fitness**: bright orange/red + white (energy, action)
- **Tech**: white/grey + electric blue (clean, modern)
- **Finance education**: dark green + white (authority, trust)
- **Lifestyle**: warm tones + soft contrasts (approachable, relatable)

Contrast rule: text must have at least 4.5:1 contrast ratio against the background. When in doubt, add a semi-transparent box behind the text.

### 4 — Facial Expression Direction
If the creator's face appears in the thumbnail:

| Expression | Use When |
|---|---|
| Shock / wide eyes | Revealing information, "you won't believe" angle |
| Pointing at text | Directing viewer attention to the hook |
| Confident smile | Positive outcome, aspiration angle |
| Serious / focused | Authority, analysis, professional tone |
| Questioning / skeptical | Comparison, honest review, "is it worth it" angle |

The expression must match the video's emotional promise. A mismatch erodes trust.

### 5 — Safe Zone Rules
YouTube overlays a video duration badge in the bottom-right corner and platform UI can crop edges. Keep all critical elements — text, face, key visuals — within these safe zones:

- **Top margin**: 5% from top edge
- **Bottom margin**: 15% from bottom edge (duration badge area)
- **Left/right margins**: 5% from each side
- **Total safe area**: center 90% of the thumbnail width, top 85% of height

### 6 — Mobile Preview Check
Before finalizing the brief, mentally preview the thumbnail at small size:
- Can you read the text without zooming?
- Is the face or key visual clearly identifiable?
- Does the color contrast hold at small size?
- Does the thumbnail communicate its topic without the title visible?

If any answer is no — revise the brief.

### 7 — A/B Variant (Optional)
For high-priority or underperforming videos, specify an A/B variant:
- Same keyword and title
- Different layout, expression, color emphasis, or text angle
- Document what hypothesis the variant is testing
