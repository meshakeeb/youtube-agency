# Audience Research

## When to Use

Activate this skill when:
- A new client's Niche Intelligence Profile has been approved and the Audience Persona Card does not yet exist
- A client is targeting a new audience segment
- The Title Copywriter or Thumbnail Strategist flags that existing persona guidance feels misaligned with video performance

## Inputs Required

- Approved Niche Intelligence Profile for the client
- Access to the client's video comments section (last 20–30 videos)
- Client's self-described audience (from onboarding intake)

## Process

### Step 1 — Classify Knowledge Level
Determine the primary viewer's knowledge level in this niche:

- **Beginner**: searches "how to start", "what is", "for beginners" — needs definitions, reassurance, simple frameworks
- **Intermediate**: searches "how to improve", "mistakes to avoid", "strategy" — needs refinement and nuance
- **Advanced**: searches "deep dive", "case study", "expert opinion" — needs specificity and data

Most channels serve one primary level with some crossover. Document the primary level and the crossover range.

Knowledge level determines: vocabulary complexity, assumed context, content depth, and pacing expectations.

### Step 2 — Map Primary Viewer Intent
Classify the dominant reason viewers come to this channel:

- **Learn**: they want to understand something they don't know yet
- **Decide**: they are choosing between options and need comparison
- **Validate**: they already have a view and want confirmation
- **Entertain**: they enjoy the niche as a hobby or spectator

One channel can serve multiple intents. Rank them by dominance. Intent drives title construction — a "learn" viewer clicks on "How to..." while a "validate" viewer clicks on "Why X is actually..."

### Step 3 — Identify Click Triggers
From the comment section and top-performing video titles, identify:

**Emotional triggers**: fear of missing out, fear of loss, desire for status, curiosity, aspiration, frustration relief
**Rational triggers**: time savings, money savings, competitive edge, clarity, simplicity

Document the top 3 emotional and top 2 rational triggers for this specific audience.

### Step 4 — Build Language Rules
Extract from the comment section and top-performing videos:

- Words and phrases the audience uses naturally (pull real examples)
- Words that resonate and repeat across comments
- Words or tones that attract complaints or negative reactions
- Formality level: how does the audience talk to each other?

### Step 5 — Document Platform Behavior
- When does this audience typically watch? (commute, lunch break, evening)
- How do they discover new videos? (search-first or browse/recommendations)
- Do they binge-watch or watch standalone videos?
- How long do they stay? (check AVD relative to video length for existing content)

## Output Format

```
AUDIENCE PERSONA CARD
Client: [Name]
Persona Name: [give the persona a human name, e.g. "Beginner Bashir"]
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

KNOWLEDGE LEVEL
Primary: [Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced]
Crossover: [secondary level if applicable]
Implication: [1–2 sentences on how this affects content framing]

PRIMARY INTENT
Ranked: [Learn / Decide / Validate / Entertain] — [2nd], [3rd]
Implication: [how this shapes title structure and hook style]

CLICK TRIGGERS
Emotional (top 3): [trigger], [trigger], [trigger]
Rational (top 2): [trigger], [trigger]

LANGUAGE RULES
Use: [words / phrases that resonate]
Avoid: [words / tones that alienate]
Formality: [casual / conversational / professional / authoritative]
Real examples from comments: [2–3 direct quotes]

PLATFORM BEHAVIOR
Watch timing: [when they watch]
Discovery mode: [search-first / browse / both]
Watch pattern: [standalone / binge]
Retention expectation: [short / medium / long-form preference]

PERSONA SUMMARY
[3–4 sentence description of this viewer as a human being]
```

## Handoff

Deliver to:
1. Chief Strategy Officer for review
2. Title Copywriter (mandatory before any title writing)
3. Thumbnail Strategist (mandatory before any thumbnail brief)
4. Description Writer (tone and language reference)

## Quality Check

- [ ] Knowledge level clearly defined with framing implication stated
- [ ] All four intent types ranked
- [ ] At least 3 emotional and 2 rational click triggers identified
- [ ] Language rules include real comment examples
- [ ] Persona summary reads like a real human, not a demographic label
