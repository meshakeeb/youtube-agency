# Community Engagement Design

## When to Use

Activate this skill immediately after a video is published — not before. Engagement strategy is built around the specific video content and should never be pre-written generically.

## Inputs Required

- Published video (title, description, chapters — the full SEO package)
- Brand Voice Profile (all community content must sound like the creator)
- Audience Persona Card (what language drives this audience to respond)

## Why the First 48 Hours Are Critical

YouTube's algorithm heavily weights engagement signals in the first 24–48 hours after upload. This period determines whether the video gets pushed to Browse and Suggested feeds — which can multiply views 5–10x beyond the initial subscriber notification.

Engagement signals the algorithm looks for:
- **Comments**: especially new comments, not just likes
- **Likes**: like-to-view ratio signals quality
- **Shares**: external shares suggest the content has value worth spreading
- **Saves**: playlist saves suggest intent to re-watch or share later

The pinned comment is the most reliable tool to trigger comments in the first 48 hours.

## Component 1 — Pinned Comment Script

The pinned comment is the first thing many viewers see after watching. It should:
- Be in the creator's voice (match Brand Voice Profile exactly)
- Ask a specific, easy-to-answer question about the video content
- Invite viewers to share their experience or opinion
- Be short enough to read in under 5 seconds
- Not feel like a corporate CTA ("Please like and subscribe!")

### Pinned Comment Formulas

**The Direct Question**:
```
"What's your biggest challenge with [video topic]? Drop it below — I read every comment."
```

**The Poll Question**:
```
"Quick question: are you currently [Option A] or [Option B]? Let me know below 👇"
```

**The Experience Invite**:
```
"Have you tried [main technique from video]? What happened? Share your result below."
```

**The Prediction Challenge**:
```
"Before watching: write down what you think [outcome] will be. Let's see if the video changes your mind."
```

Apply Brand Voice ALWAYS/NEVER rules to every draft.

## Component 2 — YouTube Community Post

YouTube Community posts appear in the subscriber feed and can drive significant early views. Post timing: within 1–2 hours of video upload for maximum algorithm benefit.

### Community Post Structure
```
[Hook — 1 sentence that creates curiosity about the video]
[Value teaser — what the viewer will learn, no more than 2 sentences]
[Link to video]
[Optional: question to drive comments on the community post itself]
```

### Community Post Tones by Niche

**Trading / Finance** (professional, direct):
```
New video just dropped. This week I break down the exact setup I used on
EUR/USD last Tuesday — the one most traders miss completely.

If you trade price action, this is worth 12 minutes of your time.

[LINK]

What's your preferred pair right now?
```

**Fitness** (energetic, motivational):
```
5 workouts. No equipment. 20 minutes each. Posted.

This is the routine I've been running every morning for 6 weeks —
here's exactly what changed.

[LINK]

Are you doing morning or evening workouts right now?
```

## Component 3 — In-Video Verbal Engagement Hooks

These are not written text — they are suggested lines for the creator to deliver verbally during recording or as notes for future videos. They are placed at specific moments:

### Hook Placement Guide

**Early hook (30–90 seconds in)**: Sets the expectation of engagement
- "Comment below right now — before you finish watching..."
- "I want to know [specific question] — put your answer in the comments."

**Mid-video hook (50–60% through the video)**: Re-engages viewers who are still watching
- "Quick question before I continue — [question related to the current topic]"
- "If this is making sense, give it a thumbs up so I know I'm on the right track."

**End hook (final 30 seconds)**: Drives comment action at close
- "What was your biggest takeaway from this video? Tell me in the comments."
- "Try [specific thing from video] this week and report back — I'll be reading every comment."

## Handoff

Engagement Package → Client (for immediate post-publish action)
Engagement Package → Client Reporting Agent (logged for attribution tracking)

## Quality Check

- [ ] Pinned comment asks a specific, easy-to-answer question
- [ ] Pinned comment is under 2 sentences
- [ ] Community post has hook + value teaser + link (+ optional question)
- [ ] All 3 in-video hook placements specified
- [ ] Brand Voice ALWAYS/NEVER rules applied to all components
- [ ] Timing note included for community post
