# Content Repurposing

## When to Use

Activate this skill after a video is published and the full SEO package is delivered. Runs in parallel with Community Engagement Design — both are post-publish Growth Team outputs.

## Inputs Required

- Published video package (title, description, chapter structure)
- Video summary or script (from client — ideally the full script or detailed outline)
- Brand Voice Profile (tone and vocabulary for each platform)
- List of platforms the client actively uses (confirm at onboarding)

## Platform-by-Platform Production Guide

---

### Platform 1 — YouTube Shorts (3 scripts per video)

**Format**: Vertical video, 60 seconds maximum, hook must land in first 3 seconds.

**Shorts Extraction Strategy**: Identify the 3 most self-contained, high-value moments in the chapter structure — moments that:
- Make a strong point without needing prior context
- Have a clear beginning and end within 60 seconds
- Work with or without the full video

**Shorts Script Structure**:
```
[HOOK — 3 seconds, single punchy statement or question]
[CORE — 40–50 seconds, the key insight or demonstration]
[CTA — 5–7 seconds, drive to full video]
```

**Shorts Hook Formulas**:
- "Here's the one thing 90% of [niche audience] get wrong about [topic]..."
- "This [technique/setup/tip] changed how I [outcome]..."
- "Stop doing [common mistake]. Here's why..."
- "In 60 seconds: [the main point of the full video]"

**CTA for Shorts**:
- "Full breakdown is on the channel — link in bio."
- "Watch the full video for the complete [strategy/guide/breakdown]."

---

### Platform 2 — Twitter/X Thread (6–10 tweets)

**Format**: Thread starting with a strong hook tweet, followed by insight tweets, ending with a value summary and link.

**Thread Structure**:
```
Tweet 1 (HOOK): Bold claim, counterintuitive insight, or surprising result
Tweet 2: Context — why this matters
Tweets 3–7: Core insights, one per tweet. Each tweet = one standalone insight.
Tweet 8: The "if you only remember one thing" summary tweet
Tweet 9: Link to full video + clear CTA
Tweet 10 (optional): Engagement question
```

**Tweet Rules**:
- Each tweet must work as a standalone insight (not cliff-hangers that require the next tweet)
- 240 character limit per tweet
- No more than 1 image per 3 tweets (keep it text-dominant)
- Match the creator's Twitter voice — may be more informal than their YouTube voice

**Example Thread Opening** (trading niche):
```
Tweet 1: "Most traders lose money not because of bad entries, but because of bad exits.

Here's the exit framework I've used for 3 years: 🧵"

Tweet 2: "First — why exits matter more than entries.

Your entry determines your risk. Your exit determines your profit.

Most trading education focuses on entries. That's backwards."
```

---

### Platform 3 — LinkedIn Post

**Format**: Single post, professional framing, 150–300 words. LinkedIn rewards longer, insight-dense posts.

**LinkedIn Adaptation Rules**:
- More professional tone than YouTube or Twitter — even if the creator is casual on other platforms
- Lead with a professional insight or data point, not entertainment
- Frame the video's content as a business or career lesson where possible
- End with a thoughtful question that invites professional comment
- Include the video link with context ("I unpacked this in full on YouTube — link below")

**LinkedIn Structure**:
```
[Hook line — bold claim or professional insight, 1 sentence]
[Context — why this matters professionally, 2–3 sentences]
[3–5 bullet points of key takeaways from the video]
[Video link with framing]
[Engagement question]
```

---

### Platform 4 — Instagram Caption

**Format**: 150–300 characters for the first line (appears before "more"), full caption up to 2,200 characters but first line must hook.

**Instagram Adaptation Rules**:
- More visual and emotional than Twitter — Instagram is an aspirational platform
- First line must create curiosity or emotion without requiring context
- Use line breaks frequently — Instagram captions are read in short bursts
- Include 5–10 relevant hashtags at the bottom (Instagram hashtags still work)
- CTA directs to YouTube via "link in bio"

**Instagram Structure**:
```
[First line hook — curiosity or emotion, max 150 chars]

[2–3 short paragraphs of value]

[CTA — "Full video on YouTube — link in bio 🎬"]

[Hashtags — 5–10 relevant]
```

---

### Platform 5 — Telegram/Discord Announcement

**Format**: Short, community-feel announcement. Feels like a message from a friend, not a marketing post.

**Telegram/Discord Rules**:
- Most informal of all platforms — community members expect a personal tone
- Keep it short: 3–5 sentences maximum
- Lead with what's new and why it matters to this community specifically
- Include a direct video link (not a "link in bio" redirect)
- Optional: exclusive insight or comment that isn't in the video (rewards community members)

**Template**:
```
🎬 New video just dropped.

[1 sentence on what the video covers]
[1 sentence on why this community specifically should watch it]

[Direct YouTube link]

[Optional: "One thing I didn't say in the video: [exclusive insight]"]
```

## Handoff

Repurposing Package → Client (for publishing on their platforms)
Repurposing Package → Client Reporting Agent (logged for attribution)

## Quality Check

- [ ] 3 Shorts scripts with hook, core, and CTA
- [ ] Shorts hooks land within 3 seconds
- [ ] Twitter thread is 6–10 tweets, each standalone
- [ ] LinkedIn post has professional framing and ends with question
- [ ] Instagram first line hooks within 150 characters
- [ ] Telegram/Discord message is brief and community-toned
- [ ] Brand Voice ALWAYS/NEVER rules applied across all platforms
- [ ] All platforms included that are active for this client
