# Niche Mapping

## When to Use

Activate this skill when:
- A new client has been onboarded and no Niche Intelligence Profile exists yet
- A client's niche has shifted significantly (new sub-niche, new audience target)
- A quarterly niche refresh is due
- The Competitor Analyst or Keyword Researcher flags that niche signals have changed

Do NOT activate for individual video production runs unless the topic falls outside the existing niche profile.

## Inputs Required

- Client name and channel URL
- Client's stated niche (e.g. "forex trading for beginners", "home fitness for busy moms")
- Any existing niche profile to refresh (if applicable)

## Process

### Step 1 — Define the Niche Boundary
Confirm the niche at two levels:
- **Broad niche**: the parent category (e.g. personal finance, fitness, tech)
- **Specific niche**: the exact lane the client operates in (e.g. forex day trading, HIIT for women over 40)

Document both. The specific niche drives all keyword and content decisions.

### Step 2 — Map the Top 10 Channels
Identify the 10 most relevant competing or adjacent channels. For each, document:
- Channel name and URL
- Subscriber count and approximate monthly view volume
- Upload frequency and average video length
- Content style: educational / entertainment / hybrid
- Top 3 performing video topics
- Obvious strengths and observable weaknesses

### Step 3 — Extract Niche Vocabulary
Build the niche vocabulary list — the actual language the audience uses:
- Primary search terms (how viewers find content in this niche)
- Community jargon and insider language
- Terms that signal beginner vs. advanced viewer
- Terms the niche avoids or rejects

### Step 4 — Identify Trending Angles
Survey the last 90 days of uploads in the niche:
- What topics are getting unusually high views relative to channel size?
- Are there emerging formats (reaction, case study, ranked list) gaining traction?
- Are there seasonal content patterns (e.g. "tax season" for finance niches)?

### Step 5 — Initial Content Gap Observations
Note obvious gaps for the Competitor Analyst to investigate further:
- Topics that are searched but have no strong videos covering them
- Content formats that are absent from the niche's top channels
- Audience questions in comments that no video has answered well

## Handoff

Deliver the completed Niche Intelligence Profile to:
1. Chief Strategy Officer for review and approval
2. Audience Persona Builder (for persona layering)
3. Competitor Analyst (gap observations as starting brief)
4. Production Team (reference file for all video work)

## Quality Check

Before handing off, confirm:
- [ ] Both niche levels defined
- [ ] All 10 channels documented with weaknesses noted
- [ ] Vocabulary list has at least 15 terms
- [ ] At least 3 trending angles identified
- [ ] At least 3 gap observations passed to Competitor Analyst
