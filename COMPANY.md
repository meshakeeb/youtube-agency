# YouTube Agency

## Mission

To be the most effective AI-powered YouTube growth agency in the world — one that treats every creator's channel like a business, not a hobby. We exist to turn uploaded videos into discovered, watched, and shared content at scale.

## Vision

A world where any creator — trading educator, fitness coach, travel vlogger, or tech reviewer — can compete at the highest level of their niche without needing a full in-house marketing team. YouTube Agency is their unfair advantage.

## Company Boundaries

YouTube Agency operates exclusively in the YouTube creator economy. Our scope is:

- **In scope**: YouTube SEO, video content strategy, channel architecture, audience growth, cross-platform repurposing, client onboarding and reporting.
- **Out of scope**: Video production, filming, editing, paid advertising management, or platform work outside YouTube and its adjacent social channels.

We serve creators across all niches. Every client gets a dedicated niche intelligence profile, a brand voice profile, and a full SEO production pipeline tailored to their audience.

## Company Goals

1. **$1M ARR within 24 months** — achieved by onboarding 50+ clients at a $1,500–$3,000/month retainer tier.
2. **Client retention above 85%** — driven by measurable channel growth, transparent reporting, and consistent output quality.
3. **Average client channel growth of 40% within 90 days** — measured by views, CTR improvement, and subscriber velocity.
4. **Zero generic output** — every deliverable is niche-specific, brand-voice-approved, and quality-controlled before delivery.
5. **Reusable operating structure** — all skills, agent instructions, and workflows are packaged and portable across clients and runtimes.

## Operating Principles

- **Niche first, always.** No keyword research, title, or strategy begins before a niche intelligence profile exists for that client.
- **Brand voice is non-negotiable.** Every output passes through brand voice review. Clients reject content that doesn't sound like them.
- **Data beats opinion.** Strategy is driven by search volume, competitor gaps, and performance metrics — not gut feel.
- **Progressive quality gates.** Work flows through Intelligence → Production → Quality Control → Delivery. Nothing ships without QC sign-off.
- **Transparency builds retention.** Every client receives weekly and monthly performance reports with clear attribution of results.
